Services

Hands-on care for the problems that bring people in

Every visit starts with a conversation and an exam, not an assumption. Treatment is manual chiropractic care, adapted to how your body actually responds.

Chiropractic Adjustment

Precise manual adjustment of the spine and joints to restore motion where things have locked up. Technique and force are matched to your age, build and comfort — gentler mobilisation is always an option.

Low Back Pain & Sciatica

For the ache that won't settle and the leg pain that follows it. Care focuses on the lumbar spine, pelvis and hips, with simple guidance on sitting, lifting and sleeping so you stop re‑triggering it.

Neck Pain & Tension Headaches

Stiff necks, limited turning, and the headaches that start at the base of the skull. Treatment addresses the upper spine and surrounding muscle tension together, because one keeps feeding the other.

Posture & Ergonomic Guidance

If your pain lives at a desk or a steering wheel, the fix has to follow you there. You'll get specific, unfussy changes to your chair, screen, phone habits and a short set of movements worth actually doing.

Auto Injury & Whiplash

Collisions on the 170 and 101 rarely hurt on day one. If your neck, shoulders or mid‑back tightened up after a crash, an early exam matters — you'll be told plainly whether imaging or a referral is the better first step.

Maintenance & Wellness Visits

For long‑standing patients who know their body and want to stay ahead of a flare‑up. Occasional check‑ins, scheduled around your life — not a prepaid plan you have to use up.

About the practice

A small practice, run the old-fashioned way

Krpan Margaret DC is a licensed Doctor of Chiropractic practising in Valley Village. The practice is deliberately small and now runs part-time — which means fewer appointments each week, but far more attention inside each one.

There's no front-desk script here and no upsell. You'll describe what hurts, be examined properly, and hear an honest opinion about whether chiropractic care is likely to help. When something looks like it belongs with a physician, an imaging centre or a physical therapist, you'll be told that instead.

Many patients have been coming for years and now bring their adult children. If that's the kind of care you're looking for — unhurried, familiar, and straight with you — this is a good fit.

Your first visit

Exactly what to expect

No mystery, no surprise add-ons. A first appointment usually runs about 45 minutes.

  1. 1

    You talk, she listens

    Where it hurts, when it started, what makes it worse, and what you've already tried. Bring any prior imaging or reports if you have them handy.

  2. 2

    A proper exam

    Posture, range of motion, and hands-on assessment of the spine and joints involved — so treatment targets the actual restriction rather than just the sore spot.

  3. 3

    An honest plan

    You'll hear what's likely going on, roughly how many visits it tends to take, and whether a referral elsewhere would serve you better. Then you decide.

  4. 4

    Treatment & homework

    Manual adjustment or gentler mobilisation as appropriate, plus one or two things to do at home. Small, specific, and realistic.
